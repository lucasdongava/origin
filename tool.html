What has to work?

The comparison runs in your browser. We do not save any data: what you paste is not stored or sent to any server, and it disappears when you close or reload this page. Screenshots are read on your device too, and the image is never uploaded. Reading text from an image can make mistakes, so always check the result. Before deleting a record in production, check with whoever manages the domain.
